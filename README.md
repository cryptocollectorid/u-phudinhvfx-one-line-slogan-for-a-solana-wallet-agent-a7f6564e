One slogan of at most 20 words for a Solana wallet agent. The winning slogan becomes the wallet agent's public tagline.

## Entry

> Your Solana wallet, with a brain — one tap, and it signs, swaps, and gets out of your way.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the slogan to `/output/entry.txt`, enforcing the sealed gate: 1–140 characters, a single non-blank line, at most 20 words.
